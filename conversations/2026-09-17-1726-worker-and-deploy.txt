  The stuck-after-failure bug

  I think I found the actual mechanism, and it's client-side. takeTurn had no
  timeout anywhere. If a stream stalls — connection dropped without closing,
  Worker dies mid-stream — reader.read() never resolves and never rejects. So
  busy stays true, sync() keeps Send disabled, and the only way out is a reload.
  That matches your symptom exactly, and it would happen regardless of what
  caused the underlying failure.

  Two watchdogs now guarantee the turn always ends: a 90s stall timer reset by
  every incoming event, and a 300s absolute ceiling. Both abort the fetch, which
  rejects the read, runs the catch, and hits the finally that restores busy = 
  false. The message tells you nothing was drawn and to try again.

  Clearing the canvas now resets

  Undo everything and the transcripts, log, and status reset with it. Previously
  the reasoning panel kept text from a drawing that no longer existed, and the
  next turn read as a continuation of it.
